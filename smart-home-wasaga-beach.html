Seasonal and second homes

A home that sits empty for part of the year needs to look after itself. I set up low-temperature alerts on your thermostat, leak sensors where water can escape, and a doorbell or camera you can check from your phone, so a furnace problem or a burst pipe is something you hear about right away rather than discover in the spring.
