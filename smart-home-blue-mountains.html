The Blue Mountains is a mix of year-round homes, weekend chalets and short-term rentals, from Thornbury and Clarksburg to Craigleith and the area around Blue Mountain Village. That mix creates specific technology needs. Owners want to know the property is warm, dry and secure mid-week. Hosts need entry, Wi-Fi and TVs that work for every guest without a call at 10 PM.

I install and set up smart home technology across The Blue Mountains, and I set it up with winter in mind: locks and doorbells that hold up in the cold, thermostats that alert you before pipes are at risk, and Wi-Fi strong enough to reach every level of a large chalet.

Short-term rental owners

If you rent your property for less than 30 days at a time, the Town of The Blue Mountains requires a short-term accommodation licence. Licensing is between you and the Town. What I handle is the technology that keeps guests happy and your property protected: scheduled door codes, a solid guest Wi-Fi network, TVs and streaming that just work, and alerts if the heat or water fails between stays.
