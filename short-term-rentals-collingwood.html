Ask About Short-Term Rental Tech Support

Frequently asked questions

Can guest door codes change for each booking?

Yes. I set up scheduled codes that start and expire on the dates you choose, so past guests can't get back in.

Do you prepare rentals before the busy season?

Yes. I check Wi-Fi, entry, TVs and streaming, cameras and smart devices before busy periods so the first guests of the season don't find the problems.

Can you write instructions for my guests?

Yes. I create simple guides for Wi-Fi, TV, streaming and entry so guests can help themselves instead of messaging you.

Do you handle short-term rental licensing?

No. Licensing is handled by your municipality. In The Blue Mountains, rentals of less than 30 days need a short-term accommodation licence from the Town. I take care of the technology side.
