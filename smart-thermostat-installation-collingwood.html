Frequently asked questions

Will a smart thermostat work with my older furnace?

In most cases, yes. Some systems need a C-wire adapter, which I can install as part of the job. I confirm compatibility before any work begins.

How long does installation take?

Most smart thermostat installations take one to two hours, including setup and a walkthrough of the app.

Which smart thermostats do you install?

I install Google Nest, Ecobee and Honeywell Home thermostats, among others, and recommend the one that suits your heating system and how you use your home.

Can I get an alert if my vacation property gets too cold?

Yes. Most smart thermostats can send a low-temperature alert to your phone. I set the threshold with you so you have time to act before pipes are at risk.
