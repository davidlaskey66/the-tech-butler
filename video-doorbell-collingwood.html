Frequently asked questions

Should I get a wired or battery video doorbell?

Wired doorbells have steady power and never need charging. Battery models work where there is no wiring, but cold weather shortens battery life. I recommend the right one for your door.

Will a video doorbell work with my existing chime?

Often, yes. I check transformer voltage and chime compatibility first. Some setups need a power kit, which I can fit during the visit.

Do I need a subscription?

Many video doorbells need a subscription to save recordings. I explain the options and trade-offs so you can decide before anything is set up.

Ring or Google Nest: which is better?

Both work well. The better choice usually depends on which smart home system you already use. My Ring vs Nest guide compares them in detail.
