Common Technology Problems We Fix

Most people do not need more technology. They need the technology they already have to work properly, reliably, and simply.

Wi-Fi dead zones

Weak or unreliable Wi-Fi in parts of the home, especially where you need it most.

Devices that do not work together

Smart home products installed over time that never got configured into one simple system.

TVs and streaming frustration

Confusing remotes, wrong inputs, poor setup, or streaming services that are difficult to access.

Disconnecting cameras, locks, and doorbells

Devices that drop offline, fail intermittently, or stop working the way they should.

Too many apps and settings

A pile of apps, accounts, remotes, and settings that has become difficult to manage.

New devices never set up properly

Equipment purchased with good intentions but never fully installed, configured, tested, or explained.
