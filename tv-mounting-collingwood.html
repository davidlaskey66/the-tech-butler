Frequently asked questions

How long does TV mounting take?

Most TV mounting jobs take one to two hours, depending on the wall, the mount and how much cable work is involved.

Do you supply the TV mount and cables?

I can supply the right mount and cables, or you can buy them yourself. Either way, I recommend the mount type that suits your TV size and wall.

Can you mount a TV above a fireplace?

Often, yes. Heat, viewing height and what is inside the wall all matter, so I check those before anything is drilled and tell you plainly if another spot would work better.

Can you hide the cables?

Yes. I route power and signal cables so they are concealed, using methods that respect fire separation and leave access for future upgrades.
