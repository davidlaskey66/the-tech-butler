Frequently asked questions

Should I use cloud or local recording?

Cloud recording keeps footage off-site and is simple to use. Local recording avoids monthly fees. We decide together based on how you want to review events.

How many security cameras do I need?

It depends on your entries, driveway and any outbuildings. I plan coverage around how people actually approach your property rather than a fixed number.

Do security cameras work in an Ontario winter?

Yes, when you choose weather-rated models and mount them sensibly, for example under eaves. I note any spots where lenses may need seasonal attention.

Can I watch my cameras from my phone while I'm away?

Yes. I set up mobile access for the right family members and test live view and playback on your phone before I leave.
