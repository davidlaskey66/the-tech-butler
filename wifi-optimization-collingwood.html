Frequently asked questions

Do I need mesh Wi-Fi?

If a single router can't cover your floors, thick walls or a detached garage, mesh Wi-Fi usually solves it. I assess your home before recommending anything.

Will mesh Wi-Fi work with my Bell or Rogers internet?

Yes. Mesh systems work alongside your provider's modem. I configure them together so you get one reliable network.

Can I have a separate guest network?

Yes. I set up guest access so visitors and rental guests can get online without access to your own devices.

Why do my cameras and doorbell keep going offline?

Weak Wi-Fi at the edges of the home is the most common cause. Fixing coverage usually fixes the devices.
